Title: Workspaces and layouts

# Workspaces and layouts | One for work. *One for Sunday.*

A workspace is a whole page of its own: its cards, their layout, the view, the date and the background. Make one for every mood, then move between them in a click. Everything you change is kept in the workspace you're in, so going back finds it as you left it.

## Switching | Point, slide, *click.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/ws-switch.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/ws-switch.jpg"></video>

The workspace's name heads the page at the top right. Point at it and the other workspaces slide out beside it, then a **+** for a new one; click one to switch. Rest on a name for a second to see a picture of that workspace.

Each workspace keeps its own cards, layout, view (tiles or columns), date setting, background and look. Note locations, weather and task settings are shared by all of them.

## The picker | All your rooms, *at a glance.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/ws-picker.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/ws-picker.jpg"></video>

Click the workspace's name to open the picker: every workspace as a picture of its page, as you last left it, in a row you can narrow by typing.

Right-click the name, use the page's right-click menu, or run **Switch workspace**, **New workspace** (an empty page), **Duplicate workspace**, **Rename workspace** and **Delete workspace**. **Move to workspace…**, on a card's right-click menu, sends the card to another workspace.

## Layouts | Same cards, *another arrangement.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/ws-layouts.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/ws-layouts.jpg"></video>

Each workspace can keep several layouts of the same cards: one for focus, one for planning. A layout holds the view, where each card sits, column widths, card heights and which cards are folded. The cards and their settings are shared, so a card added or removed in one layout is added to or removed from them all.

Point at the workspace's name to see its layouts in a small row under it, named 1, 2 and so on. Click one to switch, the cards sliding to their new places; click the open one to rename it; click **+** to start a new one as a copy of the one you're in. **Next layout**, **Previous layout**, **New layout**, **Rename layout** and **Delete layout** are on the row's right-click menu and in the command palette.

## Daily notes per workspace | Separate days, *if you like.*

Daily notes can be shared by every workspace, kept where **Where daily notes live** says (Obsidian's Daily notes or Periodic Notes settings, or Atrium's own), or kept per workspace.

**Daily notes per workspace…**, in the workspace menu, the Daily and Monthly cards' settings or as a command, lists every workspace with a folder for its daily and monthly notes and, if you like, a template of its own. The date format stays shared. Each workspace needs a folder before it can be saved, and a new workspace asks for one.

Everything Atrium writes to or reads from the daily note then uses the open workspace's: the Daily and Monthly cards, the agenda, Recorder, Music, Podcasts and timer logs, habits, the time machine and saved weather. A card still running after you switch, such as Music playing, keeps writing to its own workspace's note. Tasks and Upcoming leave out other workspaces' daily and monthly notes.

Switching between shared and per workspace moves nothing; changing a workspace's folder later offers to move its notes. Obsidian's own daily note command and other plugins keep using the shared settings.
