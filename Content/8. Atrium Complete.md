# Atrium Complete | $14.99, *once.*

Atrium is free to use. **Atrium Complete** adds the Focus, Time machine, Terminal, Recorder, Music, Web page, Podcasts and Habits cards, and video backgrounds. No subscription in sight.

## Try it | Thirty days, *on the house.*

The Complete cards start locked. **Start 30-day trial** opens a free checkout at Polar, which asks for your email and gives you a trial key; paste it into Atrium, and the cards unlock for 30 days. After that, they need a license key: $14.99 once, yours for life, from [Polar](https://buy.polar.sh/polar_cl_y0kZRpPkmgPvNxV0jekPBHkZdU4a5oBNyric248vSAY?discount_code=EARLYBIRD). The first 100 keys are free with the code `EARLYBIRD`, filled in by that link.

**Start 30-day trial** and **Enter license key** are in Atrium's settings, on a locked card, on its right-click menu and as commands. Without a key, every other card keeps working as before.

## Your key | Three devices, *for life.*

Entering the key activates it on that device, once, online; a key works on up to three devices, and Atrium then works offline.

A few parts depend on the system: the Terminal card works on Mac and Linux, not yet on Windows; the Recorder records everywhere but dictates only on macOS 26 or later; and the agenda's Calendar events are Mac only. The trial is the way to check before buying.
