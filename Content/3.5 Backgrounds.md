Title: Backgrounds

# Backgrounds | A fire, rain on the glass, *a café in Paris.*

Behind your cards goes anything you like: a picture, a video of your own, or one of those cozy ambiance videos people leave running for hours. Each workspace keeps its own.

## Paste a video | Cozy, *in one paste.*

<video src="/_assets/ws-the-reader.mp4" poster="/_assets/ws-the-reader.jpg"></video>

Find a fireplace, rain on a window, a snowy cabin or a café's murmur on YouTube, copy its address, and paste it into **Change background…**. It plays behind your cards straight from YouTube, looping, with nothing downloaded and nothing to set up.

**Change background…** is on the page's right-click menu, in Atrium's settings under Appearance, and a command. Videos you've pasted are kept in a note, **Background videos**, so the picker offers them again next time.

- **Sound:** turn off **Mute background** for the crackle and the rain, and set **Background volume** to taste.
- **Speed:** **Video speed** slows a video down for something calmer, or speeds it up.

## From your vault | Your pictures, *your clips.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/bg-picker.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/bg-picker.jpg"></video>

The picker shows the images and videos in your vault, or only those in the **Background folder** set in Atrium's settings, in a row you scroll through, each video playing as you reach it. Type to narrow them down; "video" finds the videos.

Paste the address of a picture or video file from the web and Atrium downloads it into your vault and uses it. A web page's address gives its video, or else its largest picture.

Set **Background image (light mode)** in settings for a separate picture in light mode; without one, the dark mode picture shows in both. **None** shows no background at all.

## The look | Cards *over the view.*

- **Tone down background** dims the picture, or in light mode washes it out, so the cards stand out.
- **Background blur** softens the whole picture.
- **Card opacity** lets the background show through the cards, and **Card blur** frosts what shows.

Pictures are free. Video backgrounds, YouTube's included, are part of [Atrium Complete](/atrium-complete).
