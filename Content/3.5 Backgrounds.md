Title: Backgrounds

# Backgrounds | A fire, rain on the glass, *a café in Paris.*

Behind your cards goes anything you like: a picture, a video of your own, or one of those cozy ambiance videos people leave running for hours. Each workspace keeps its own.

## Paste a video | Cozy, *in one paste.*

<video src="/_assets/ws-the-reader.mp4" poster="/_assets/ws-the-reader.jpg"></video>

Find a fireplace, rain on a window, a snowy cabin or a café's murmur on YouTube, copy its address, and paste it into **Change background…**. It plays behind your cards straight from YouTube, looping, with nothing downloaded and nothing to set up.

**Change background…** is on the page's right-click menu, in Atrium's settings under Appearance, a command, and <kbd>b</kbd> on the page. Videos you've pasted are kept in your Backgrounds file, so the picker offers them again next time.

- **Sound:** turn off **Mute background** for the crackle and the rain, and set **Background volume** to taste.
- **Speed:** **Video speed** slows a video down for something calmer, or speeds it up.

## Art from Wikipedia | A museum, *one search away.*

Open the picker and paintings from Wikimedia Commons are already waiting after your own backgrounds. Type a painting, a painter or a place, and Wikipedia's pictures for it follow as you pause, art first. Pick one and Atrium downloads it into your vault, named after its article, with a line crediting where it came from.

The chips under the search show or hide each kind: **Art**, **Photos**, **Other**, **Videos** and **Untagged**. <kbd>Shift</kbd> and a chip's number toggles it, holding <kbd>Shift</kbd> shows the numbers, and the picker remembers your choice.

## From your vault | Your pictures, *your clips.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/bg-picker.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/bg-picker.jpg"></video>

The picker shows your backgrounds in a row you scroll through, each video playing as you reach it. Type to narrow them down, by name or by tag; "video" finds the videos. Typing also finds any other picture or video in your vault, and picking it adds it to your backgrounds.

Paste the address of a picture or video file from the web and Atrium downloads it into your vault and uses it. A web page's address gives its video, or else its largest picture.

## The Backgrounds file | One note, *every background.*

Your backgrounds are listed in a note, **Backgrounds** (`Atrium/Backgrounds.md` unless you set another **Backgrounds file** in settings), wherever the pictures sit in your vault: a line for each, with its tags and where it came from. Downloads go into the note's folder. Add tags like `#night` or `#cozy` and type them in the picker to find those backgrounds.

**Manage backgrounds…**, on the page's right-click menu, as a command, or from the picker, opens the list as a grid over the page. Click one, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to set it as the background; <kbd>t</kbd> edits its tags, offering the ones your vault already uses; <kbd>Delete</kbd> takes it off the list and leaves the file; <kbd>n</kbd> adds a picture from your vault. Its right-click menu also deletes the file itself. Filter for "untagged" to find the ones still to tag.

Set **Background image (light mode)** in settings for a separate picture in light mode; without one, the dark mode picture shows in both. **None** shows no background at all.

## The look | Cards *over the view.*

- **Tone down background** dims the picture, or in light mode washes it out, so the cards stand out.
- **Background blur** softens the whole picture.
- **Card opacity** lets the background show through the cards, and **Card blur** frosts what shows.

Pictures are free. Video backgrounds, YouTube's included, are part of [Atrium Complete](/atrium-complete).
