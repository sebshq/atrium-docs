Title: Network use

# Network use | What leaves *your vault.*

Atrium goes online only for what you ask of it. Here's all of it.

## The list | Short, *on purpose.*

- **Weather**, once you set a location: the place goes to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) to find its coordinates and fetch the forecast. Coordinates you enter instead go to OpenStreetMap's [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org) to find the place name. Weather is off until a location is set.
- **Web page** cards load the page set in their settings, and whatever that page loads, directly from the site.
- **Podcasts** cards fetch the feeds in their subscriptions note and play episodes from where they're hosted; searching for shows sends what you type to [Podverse](https://podverse.fm).
- **Your license key** goes once to [Polar](https://polar.sh), with the kind of device (Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone or iPad, or Android), to activate it on that device.
- **On a Mac**, once for each version, Atrium downloads its two small helpers, for Calendar events and dictation, from the version's [release on GitHub](https://github.com/sebshq/obsidian-atrium/releases), as Obsidian installs only the plugin's main files.
- **Report a bug** opens an email to support@atrium-md.com in your mail app, with the modules you use filled in; nothing is sent until you send it.
- **Ask Claude Code**, if you turn it on in a Recorder card, sends what you choose to ask to Claude Code, which sends it to Anthropic as it does anything you type into it.

## Everything else | *Stays home.*

Nothing else leaves your vault. Dictation in a Recorder card is transcribed on your Mac and never sent anywhere.
