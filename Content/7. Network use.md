# Network use

If you set a weather location, Atrium sends it to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) to look up a city's coordinates and fetch the forecast. If you enter coordinates instead, they are sent to OpenStreetMap's [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org) to find the place name. A Web page card loads the page set in its settings, and whatever that page loads, directly from the site. A Podcasts card fetches the feeds in its subscriptions note and plays episodes from where they're hosted, and searching for shows sends what you type to [Podverse](https://podverse.fm). **Report a bug**, on the page's right-click menu, in settings and as a command, opens an email to support@atrium-md.com in your mail app with the modules you use filled in; nothing is sent until you send it. Entering a license key sends it once to [Polar](https://polar.sh), with the device's name, to activate it on that device. On a Mac, once for each version, Atrium downloads its two small helpers, for Calendar events and dictation, from the version's [release on GitHub](https://github.com/sebshq/obsidian-atrium/releases), as Obsidian installs only the plugin's main files. Nothing else leaves your vault, and weather is off until a location is set. Dictation in a Recorder card is transcribed on your Mac and never sent anywhere. With **Ask Claude Code** on, what you choose to ask goes to Claude Code, which sends it to Anthropic as it does anything you type into it.
