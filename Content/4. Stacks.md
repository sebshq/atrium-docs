Title: Stacks

# Stacks and floating cards | Cards, *shuffled.*

Too many cards for the room? Let several share one spot and show one at a time. Or lift one off the page and let it hover while you work around it.

## Stacking | One spot, *several cards.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/stack-make.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/stack-make.jpg"></video>

Pick out the cards (click one's frame, then <kbd>⌘</kbd>-, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>- or <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click the others) and choose **Stack these** from their right-click menu, or press <kbd>:</kbd>. Any cards can share a stack.

The card showing has a faint **+1** (or more) after its name.

## Moving through | A deck, *but tidier.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/stack-cycle.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/stack-cycle.jpg"></video>

Point at the card's header and the others' names slide out: before its name, one for each other kind of card in the stack, and after it, the other cards of its own kind, such as other notes. Click one to bring it forward.

- <kbd>}</kbd> and <kbd>{</kbd> bring the next or previous card forward.
- <kbd>⌥}</kbd> and <kbd>⌥{</kbd> move the card showing later or earlier in the stack.
- **Take out of stack**, or <kbd>⌥;</kbd>, puts the card showing beside the stack, the next one taking its place.

## Floating | Some cards *like to hover.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/float.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/float.jpg"></video>

**Float**, the header button, the card's right-click menu or <kbd>⇧F</kbd>, lifts a card off the page into a window over it, and the cards around it close up its place. Drag it by its frame to move it, and from its corner to resize it; it stays where you left it, even after Obsidian restarts.

**Put back on the page**, the same button, menu item or key, or <kbd>Esc</kbd>, returns it to its place. Drag it onto a gap between cards in columns, or near a card's edge in tiles, and it lands on the page there.
