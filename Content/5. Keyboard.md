Title: Keyboard

# Keyboard | Your mouse can take *the day off.*

Everything in Atrium has a key, and every right-click menu shows it at the item's right, in grey, so you learn them where you use them: a hotkey you've set for its command in Obsidian, or else Atrium's own. Every action is also a command, so you can give it a hotkey of your own.

## Getting around | Arrows, numbers, *Tab.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/keys-nav.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/keys-nav.jpg"></video>

Atrium starts with the page itself in focus, framed around all the cards. From there:

- <kbd>Enter</kbd> picks out the first card, and a number picks out its card. Hold <kbd>⇧</kbd> to see each card's number on its top edge.
- The arrows pick out the nearest card that way (<kbd>h</kbd> <kbd>j</kbd> <kbd>k</kbd> <kbd>l</kbd> with Vim on).
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> goes back to the card picked out before.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> in a card's editor, or a click on its frame, picks out the card; <kbd>Esc</kbd> again gives the keys back to the page.

## Shaping | Plus, *then arrows.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/keys-resize.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/keys-resize.jpg"></video>

Press <kbd>+</kbd> and a dashed frame marks the card, solid along the edges that can move, with its size. An arrow pushes the edge on that side out; <kbd>⇧</kbd> and an arrow pulls the opposite edge in. <kbd>=</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> grow or shrink it on every side. <kbd>+</kbd>, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> ends it.

<kbd>⇧</kbd> with an arrow moves the card instead, and <kbd>⇧A</kbd>, <kbd>⇧S</kbd>, <kbd>⇧J</kbd> then an arrow add, split or join on that side.

## Help | When in doubt, *press ?*

<video src="/_assets/guide/keys-help.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/keys-help.jpg"></video>

<kbd>?</kbd> shows the card's own shortcuts and the actions that apply to it, and **Show all shortcuts** at the foot shows the rest. On the page, <kbd>,</kbd> opens Atrium's settings.

## Every key | The whole *keyboard.*

With a card picked out:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Arrows (h j k l with Vim on) | Pick out the nearest card that way |
| 1–9, or Shift + 1–9 | Pick out the card with that number; hold Shift to see each card's number on its top edge. With ten or more cards, type both digits of 10 and up; a first digit on its own waits a moment, then picks out its card |
| Shift + arrows | Move the card that way: in tiles, past the tile beside it, cards side by side or stacked together moving as one, or out of its own row or stack at its edge; in columns, trading places with the nearest card |
| Shift+J, then an arrow or h j k l | In tiles, join the card with the tile that way |
| Shift+K, then an arrow or h j k l | In tiles, link the card with the tile that way, so they move and drag together |
| Shift+U | In tiles, unlink the card |
| Shift+A, then an arrow or h j k l | Add a module on that side of the card: in tiles, splitting the card's tile; in columns, above or below it, or in a new column on its left or right |
| Shift+A, then : | Add a module to the card's stack, at the end of its tabs and in front; a card on its own becomes a stack. Also **Add to stack** in the card's right-click menu, and the **Add module to card's stack** command |
| Shift+S, then an arrow or h j k l | Split the card that way, its copy going on that side |
| Shift+R | Replace the card with a module chosen from Add module, in the same place |
| Shift+P | In tiles, flip the card's row or stack: side by side becomes stacked, and stacked becomes side by side. With more cards picked out (Cmd/Ctrl- or Shift-click their frames), turns just those the other way (**Swap orientation**) |
| Shift+X | With two cards picked out, swap them |
| : | Stack the cards picked out behind the first, their tabs after its own |
| } and { | Show the next or previous card in the stack; its tabs keep their order, the one showing in its own place |
| Option+} and Option+{ | Move the card showing later or earlier in its stack's tab order (also **Move later in stack** and **Move earlier in stack** on its right-click menu) |
| Option+; (…) | Take the card out of its stack, beside it |
| Shift+B | In tiles, give the cards in the card's row or stack equal sizes; **Equal sizes for all cards**, on the page's right-click menu, does the whole page |
| Tab | Go back to the card picked out before, and Tab again to return |
| Cmd/Ctrl + Z | Undo the last change: to the layout, a card's settings, a workspace, or a note a card's action wrote; Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Z redoes it |
| Enter | Go into the card: its editor, its first text box, or a list it has |
| Space | Expand the card, or put it back |
| [ and ], t | On the daily note and agenda cards, show the day before or after, or today |
| Shift+M | Fold or unfold the card |
| f | Filter the card |
| , | Open the card's settings: up and down arrows move between settings, Tab goes into one and on to the next, Space flips a toggle, Esc comes back out (or, on a setting, cancels) and Enter saves |
| +, then arrows | Resize mode, until +, Esc or Enter: a dashed frame marks the card, solid along the edges that can move, with its size. An arrow pushes the edge on that side out, Shift + arrow pulls the opposite edge in, so the card goes the arrow's way: in tiles, the tiles beyond it give or take the room; in columns, → and ← widen and narrow its column, and ↓ and ↑ grow and shrink its height; expanded, its width and height. = and - grow or shrink the card on every side at once. **Resize step** in Atrium's settings sets how much each press changes |
| Delete or Backspace | Remove the card (its note stays) and pick out the nearest one |
| Cmd/Ctrl+W (Obsidian's close tab hotkey) | Remove the card, also while typing in it or in its page, rather than close Atrium |
| Esc | Put an expanded card back, or let the card go |

## Cards' own keys | A few *extras.*

Some cards add their own lowercase keys while picked out: the [Note](/note), [Agenda](/agenda), [Music](/music), [Podcasts](/podcasts), [Recorder](/recorder) and [Memorize](/memorize) pages list them.
