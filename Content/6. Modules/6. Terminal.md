# Terminal | A real shell, *in a card.*

For the people who never really left the command line.

## Your shell | Right *beside your notes.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/terminal.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/terminal.jpg"></video>

The Terminal card runs your login shell in the vault's folder, or a folder set in the card's settings. It works on Mac and Linux, through a small Python 3 helper or, without Python, the system's `expect`. Nothing runs until you add a Terminal card.

Resize the card and the shell follows; full-screen programs see its real size.
