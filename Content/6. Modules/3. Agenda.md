Title: Agenda

# Agenda | The day, *by the hour.*

Every task and event in the day's daily note, and those anywhere else due or scheduled that day, timed ones first. On a Mac, your Calendar's events join them.

## Views | List, timeline, *week, month.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/agenda-views.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/agenda-views.jpg"></video>

With the card picked out, <kbd>v</kbd> opens the view menu, and a view's first letter picks it: <kbd>v</kbd> <kbd>l</kbd> the list, <kbd>v</kbd> <kbd>t</kbd> the timeline, <kbd>v</kbd> <kbd>w</kbd> the week, <kbd>v</kbd> <kbd>m</kbd> the month.

On the timeline, <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> zoom out and in, and <kbd>c</kbd> scrolls to now. Drag items to move them in time.

## Items | Done, moved, *renamed.*

<kbd>Enter</kbd> goes to the first item in sight, and the arrows from item to item. On an item:

- <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens it, <kbd>x</kbd> marks it done, <kbd>e</kbd> renames it and <kbd>Delete</kbd> deletes it.
- <kbd>r</kbd> reschedules it: <kbd>t</kbd> today, <kbd>m</kbd> tomorrow, <kbd>w</kbd> next week, <kbd>d</kbd> a date.
- <kbd>⌥</kbd> and the arrows move it by a day or 15 minutes; with <kbd>⇧</kbd>, its end.
- <kbd>f</kbd> or <kbd>p</kbd> focuses it, which times it from now, <kbd>n</kbd> opens a calendar event's note, and <kbd>m</kbd> shows its menu.

Each is also a command.

## Music on the timeline | What was *playing.*

Tracks and episodes played (lines tagged `#song` or `#podcast`, as Music and Podcasts log them) sit as small icons in a lane at the timeline's right, and fold to a count at the end of the list. Point at an icon to name it, click to play it again, and right-click → **Add to timeline** to make it an item as long as it lasted.

## Mac Calendar | Your events, *with notes.*

A Calendar event the day already has a line for, at the same time and starting with its title, shows once, as that line.

An event gets a note of its own from the note button beside it, its right-click menu or <kbd>n</kbd>: `2026-10-22 11.00 Standup` for a single event, or one `Standup -R` for a repeating one, kept named and timed as the event changes in Calendar. **Create notes for new events**, in the card's settings, makes them automatically from when it's turned on.
