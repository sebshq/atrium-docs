Title: Files

# Files | Your folders, *in a card.*

A folder of your vault, one card over, run from the keyboard.

## Browse | Peek before *you open.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/files-browse.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/files-browse.jpg"></video>

The Files card shows a folder: its folders first, then its notes. The title is the folder's path, and each part of it is a link back up. Pick the folder in the card's settings, or jump to any folder with **Go to folder**.

Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on a focused card to reach its rows, then the arrows move through them. <kbd>→</kbd> goes into a folder, and <kbd>←</kbd> comes back up. <kbd>p</kbd> peeks at a note, unfurled right in the list; <kbd>→</kbd> goes into it to edit, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> brings you back to its row. <kbd>Space</kbd> previews the note in the card expanded, and <kbd>o</kbd> lists its headings under its row. <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or the arrow at the end of its row, opens the note as a card of its own; <kbd>⇧↩</kbd> then an arrow opens it on that side of the Files card.

A click on a note opens it in one card that the Files card reuses, so browsing doesn't fill the page; <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click opens a new card. A click on a folder goes into it, and the chevron at its left unfolds it in place.

Pull the list down past its top to reveal the filter, and scroll down to tuck it away again.

## Make and tidy | Without *leaving the card.*

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>n</kbd> | New note in this folder |
| <kbd>⇧N</kbd> | New folder |
| <kbd>e</kbd> | Rename |
| <kbd>d</kbd> | Duplicate, then name the copy |
| <kbd>r</kbd> | Reveal in Finder |
| <kbd>c</kbd> | Copy the path |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Delete, after asking; <kbd>Enter</kbd> confirms |
| <kbd>⌘Delete</kbd> | Delete straight away |
| <kbd>m</kbd> | The row's menu |

New note and New folder also wait below the last row, one <kbd>↓</kbd> past it, or always in sight if you turn them on in the card's settings.

## Your look | Plain, *or not.*

The card's settings add file icons, bold folders, muted files, item counts and a gap between folders and files. **Order files by property** puts the files that have it first, in its order, then the rest by name, and **Property values** shows each file's value at the right of its row. The folder rail puts a tick at the left edge for each folder above, and shows their names when you point at it.
