# Upcoming | What's *coming next.*

<img src="/_assets/guide/cards/upcoming.jpg" alt="The Upcoming card listing tasks by day">

The Upcoming card lists open tasks due in the days after today, grouped by day, by due date, or else scheduled or start date. Pair it with [Tasks](/tasks), which keeps today and before.

Its tasks take the same keys as the Tasks card's, and clicking a day's heading folds it.

## Settings | How *far ahead.*

- **Title**: the card's name.
- **Days ahead**: how many days it looks ahead, 7 to begin with.
- **Exclude tags**: hides tasks with these tags, separated by commas.
