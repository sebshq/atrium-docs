Title: Routines

# Routines | One step *at a time.*

The Routines card plays a routine, such as your morning or the end of the workday, one step at a time, each on its own timer. Each routine is a note, and each step a line in it. Every run goes into your daily note, so the agenda shows it and the card learns how long each step really takes.

## A routine | *Its own page.*

**New routine**, at the top of the list, makes one and opens it with its name ready to type. Click a routine to open it. Its name, at the top, can be changed there; **Start** is beside it, with **Duplicate**, for a copy to change, and **Open note**.

Below them, each step has its name and its length, such as `2m` or `90s`; leave the length empty for a step that runs until you mark it done. Click a name or a length to change it, drag a step by its handle to move it, or point at it and click × to remove it. **Add step** takes a name with its length, such as `Stretch 5m`. Everything is written to the routine's note as a list:

```
- Make bed 2m
- Stretch 90s
- Journal
```

## Schedule | *On its days.*

**Repeats** picks the days, or **Every day**, and a time. The routine then shows on the agenda on those days, and when its time comes, the agenda's alert has **Start**. A copy made with **Duplicate** starts with no schedule. Starting it from the agenda's right-click menu works too. The schedule is kept in the note's properties, the way Full Calendar keeps a recurring event.

## Music | *To go with it.*

**Music** takes a playlist note, a folder or a track. Starting the routine plays it on your Music card, and the end of the routine pauses it.

## Playing | Done, *then the next.*

The card shows the step, the time left, the step after it, and when you'll be done. When a step's time is up, a chime sounds and the time counts on past it until you mark it done.

- Click the time to pause, and again to go on. Point at it for a minute less or more.
- **Done** (d) and **Skip** (s) move to the next step; **Stop** (x) ends the routine.
- p pauses, and a and r add or remove a minute.

Under the step, **Notes on this step** takes what you want to remember. Type `[[` to link a note, as in the editor, so it shows in that note's backlinks.

## In the daily note | *What really happened.*

Starting a routine writes it into the daily note: its line, then a task for each step, timed as you go and as expected, with its notes under it.

```
- [x] [7:00 AM - 7:32 AM] [[Morning]]
	- [x] [7:00 AM - 7:03 AM] Make bed [planned:: 2m] [took:: 2m 40s]
		- Fresh sheets.
	- [-] [7:03 AM - 7:03 AM] Stretch [planned:: 90s] [took:: 10s]
```

A skipped step is crossed out. Cmd/Ctrl+Z after the routine ends takes back its last change.

## Usually | *Plans that fit.*

The routine's page shows what each step usually takes and how often it's skipped, from its last ten runs. When a step usually takes a quarter more or less than planned, **Use 3m** sets its length to what it really takes.
