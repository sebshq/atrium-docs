Title: Podcasts

# Podcasts | Your shows, *where you left off.*

Laid out as [Music](/music) is: the episode playing over its show's art, with tabs for **Episodes**, **Transcript** and **Shows**.

## Episodes | Newest *first.*

Episodes lists the newest of the shows in your subscriptions note (`Podcasts.md`, unless set in the card's settings). A click plays an episode from where you left off, with jumps back 15 and forward 30 seconds, a playback speed, **Mark as played**, **Download** and **Show notes**. A downloaded episode is saved beside the note, in `Podcasts/<Show>/`, and plays from there.

## Shows | Find one, *subscribe.*

Shows shows their covers; a click opens a show's episodes and its button plays the newest. Typing searches [Podverse](https://podverse.fm), and a result's button subscribes, adding its feed to the note.

## Transcript | Read *along.*

Where an episode's feed has a transcript (Podcasting 2.0), Transcript shows it, the line being spoken lit and followed. Click a line to go there.

## In your notes | Pick up *tomorrow.*

How far you got is a line in today's daily note, such as `- [8:12 AM] Listened to [Show — Episode](<address>) 23:41 / 1:02:10 #podcast`, updated as you listen and ending in ✓ once played. The last 90 days of daily notes are read back to resume.

Starting an episode pauses Music, and Music starting pauses the episode. On the card, <kbd>p</kbd> plays or pauses, and <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> jump back 15 and forward 30 seconds.
