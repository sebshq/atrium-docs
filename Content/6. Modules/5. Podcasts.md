Title: Podcasts

# Podcasts | Your shows, *where you left off.*

Laid out as [Music](/music) is: the episode playing over its show's art, with tabs for **Up next**, **Episodes** and **History**, and **Transcript** first while an episode plays.

## Episodes | Your shows, *then what's new.*

Episodes opens on a shelf of your shows' covers, from your subscriptions note (`Podcasts.md`, unless set in the card's settings), folded to one row until you click **Show all**. Under it, the newest episodes from every show. Click a cover and it travels up into a header over that show's episodes; the arrow, or <kbd>Backspace</kbd>, takes it back to its place on the shelf.

A click plays an episode from where you left off, its show's cover flying up into what's playing, with jumps back 15 and forward 30 seconds, a playback speed, **Mark as played**, **Download** and **Episode details**. A downloaded episode is saved beside the note, in `Podcasts/<Show>/`, and plays from there.

**+** on the shelf searches [Podverse](https://podverse.fm), the results taking the shelf's place; a result's button subscribes, adding its feed to the note. <kbd>Esc</kbd> brings the shelf back.

## Up next | In *order.*

Up next is what plays after this one, with how long it runs and when it would end. Press <kbd>n</kbd> on an episode to play it next or <kbd>u</kbd> to add it to the end, and a copy of it flies to the tab; or drag it onto the tab, which opens if you hold it there. Drag, or <kbd>Shift</kbd> and the arrows, to reorder.

## Transcript | Read *along.*

Where an episode's feed has a transcript (Podcasting 2.0), Transcript shows it, the line being spoken lit and followed. Click a line to go there.

## In your notes | Pick up *tomorrow.*

How far you got is a line in today's daily note, such as `- [8:12 AM] Listened to [Show — Episode](<address>) 23:41 / 1:02:10 #podcast`, updated as you listen and ending in ✓ once played. The last 90 days of daily notes are read back to resume.

Starting an episode pauses Music, and Music starting pauses the episode. On the card, <kbd>p</kbd> plays or pauses, and <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> jump back 15 and forward 30 seconds.
