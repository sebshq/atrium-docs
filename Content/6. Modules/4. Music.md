Title: Music

# Music | Your records *and* YouTube.

Plays the music files in your vault (MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV, Ogg, Opus and more) and YouTube links, side by side in one library. Albums, playlists and tracks, with covers, lyrics and a history of what you played, logged to the daily note as you listen.

## Two sources | Files you own, *links you found.*

Drop albums into your vault as folders of audio files and they show up as covers. Paste a YouTube link, a single video or a whole playlist, and it plays right alongside them; so does a link to an audio file or a stream on the web. A playlist note can mix all three. Nothing to sign in to, no subscription.

## Library | Covers first, *then the rest.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/music-play.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/music-play.jpg"></video>

The Library shows covers, recent first, then albums, playlists, notes that link to tracks (such as daily notes with what you played) and YouTube links. A cover's play button plays it; a click opens its tracks. Typing searches them and the vault's tracks, and takes a pasted link or a new playlist's name.

**Choose what to play**, the disc button in the header, shows the same covers floating over the page.

## Playing | Tabs for *everything else.*

The playlist's name sits on top, then what's playing, with tabs for **Library**, **Lyrics**, **History** (tracks played lately; a click plays one again) and **Playlist**. Under the playlist's last track, **Add a track…** finds the vault's tracks and adds one.

A link to an audio file anywhere in Atrium plays on the Music card when clicked; <kbd>⌘</kbd>-hover shows **Play** and **Play next**.

## Keys | Play, skip, *shuffle.*

On the card or in its lists:

- <kbd>p</kbd> plays or pauses; <kbd>n</kbd> or <kbd>]</kbd> and <kbd>[</kbd> go to the next and previous track; <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> jump ten seconds.
- <kbd>v</kbd> and <kbd>⇧V</kbd> go to the next and previous tab; <kbd>o</kbd> opens the Library to search.
- <kbd>s</kbd> shuffles, <kbd>e</kbd> repeats, and <kbd>u</kbd> tucks the tabs away under the cover or brings them back.
- In the Library, the arrows move, <kbd>Space</kbd> opens a cover's tracks and <kbd>Backspace</kbd> goes back. <kbd>Enter</kbd> plays the one at hand; <kbd>⇧Enter</kbd> puts it next.

## Any size | Small, *it keeps it short.*

Tiny, the card shows just the track playing; small, the cover beside the controls; medium, the full cover; large, the cover beside the tabs. Scrolling down over the card brings tucked tabs out, then shrinks the cover so the list has room.
