Title: Releases

# Releases | What's *new.*

Every version of Atrium, newest first, with what it added and what it fixed. Obsidian updates the plugin for you; Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates fetches a new one right away.

## 0.3.1 · 1 November 2026 | Files, *your way.*

### Features

- **Look settings for Files.** Turn on file icons, bold folders, muted files and item counts, and set the gap between rows, from the card's settings.

## 0.3.0 · 1 November 2026 | Meet *Files.*

A new module: your vault's folders in a card, run from the keyboard.

### Features

- **Files module.** A folder view with a path filter, and keys for everything: Enter opens a note as a card, p peeks at it, → goes into a note unfurled in place and Esc comes back to its row.
- **Create, rename and delete.** New note and New folder sit at the foot of the card, reachable with the arrows. Deletes ask first, and Enter confirms.
- **A trail and a rail.** The title shows the folder's path, each part clickable. The rail slides in from the left with the folder's subfolders, and Go to folder jumps anywhere.
- **Move a card within its linked group** with Shift and the arrows.

### Fixes

- Notes unfurled inside a Vim-mode note show the text cursor and the insert-mode caret.

## 0.2.0 · 1 November 2026 | Faster *agenda.*

### Features

- **Add agenda items in plain words.** Type "meeting tomorrow at 9am" and the item goes there, with a hint of where it will land. Press n to add one, and Tab to change its kind.
- **Unscheduled notes.** An agenda note with no time shows on its day as unscheduled.
- **Rename cards** and **set the page's date format.**
- **Add a module from the room under a folded tile,** as at the foot of a column.
- **Music:** open files from the library, the cover and the history menus.
- **Mac Calendar events** count in the agenda title's next event, which moves on to the following one when it starts.
- **Stacks** show their count and tabs as soon as cards are stacked.

### Fixes

- Tiles stay still when you grab a border.
- Note cards keep in step with their note when lines are removed on disk.
- With many workspaces, the date stays on one line; their names scroll beside it, with the open one kept in sight.
- The Recorder writes one time for recordings under a minute.

## 0.1.2 · 31 October 2026 | *Steadier* tiles.

### Fixes

- Tiles no longer jump when you grab a border between them.
- Style and lint cleanups for the Obsidian plugin review.

## 0.1.1 · 31 October 2026 | Small *fixes.*

### Fixes

- License activations are labelled by the kind of device (Mac, Windows, iPhone and so on).
- Settings draw without Obsidian's deprecated call.
