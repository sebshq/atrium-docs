Title: Releases

# Releases | What's *new.*

Every version of Atrium, newest first, with what it added and what it fixed. Obsidian updates the plugin for you; Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates fetches a new one right away.

## 0.8.0 · 5 November 2026 | A timeline that *keeps up.*

A look of its own for each workspace, cards that stack where you drop them, and an agenda timeline that runs past midnight and keeps your plan while you work.

### Features

- **A look per workspace.** Each workspace can turn on a CSS snippet while it's open, and set its own colour scheme for the Atrium page: Follow Obsidian, Dark or Light. See [Workspaces](/workspaces-and-layouts).
- **Stack a card by dropping it** in the middle of another; the dropped card goes in front.
- **Importing a workspace** asks for its daily folder when each workspace keeps its own.
- **Drag past midnight.** On the agenda timeline, drag a new event down into the next day; dragging near the timeline's top or foot scrolls it on into the day before or after. An overnight item's end can be dragged from its foot on the next day.
- **Timing keeps your plan.** Starting to time an item with an end time keeps that end: the block keeps its size and fills as time passes, and Stop writes the real end.
- **Checkboxes start and stop the clock.** Ticking an agenda item into in progress starts timing it, as Start does; done, cancelled or back to open stops it.

### Fixes

- <kbd>C</kbd> scrolls the timeline to the current time in one press, without the stutter.
- No more not-started reminders for calendar events, or for anything past its end time.
- The workspace switcher stays out briefly after the pointer leaves, so slipping off doesn't fold it away.

## 0.7.0 · 4 November 2026 | Rooms to *share.*

Workspaces you can pass along, rows that act at a swipe, and a Files card you can drag things around in.

### Features

- **Export and import workspaces.** **Export workspace…** saves the workspace as a file, its background images inside; **Import workspace…** adds one from a file, and an `obsidian://atrium-workspace` link adds one from the web. See [Workspaces](/workspaces-and-layouts).
- **Swipe a task or agenda item** with two fingers: to the right marks it done, to the left moves it to tomorrow, with the action's word and icon in the gap as it slides. **Swipe right** and **Swipe left**, in General and Agenda settings, set what each way does.
- **Click once to pick out, again to open.** A click picks out a task or agenda item; a click on it picked out brings up its task manager.
- **Quicker row buttons.** Pointing at a row's date or source slides in Focus, Defer to tomorrow (Start, on agenda items) and **More…**, which opens the task manager with everything else.
- **The task manager** gains Focus and timing; the agenda item menu gains Open and timing.
- **Appearance settings beside the page.** The page's appearance opens in a panel at its side, so each change shows as you make it.
- **Files card: move things.** Drag a row onto a folder, a breadcrumb, the Up button or another Files card; drop files in from Finder; <kbd>x</kbd> carries a file and <kbd>v</kbd> puts it down anywhere; <kbd>t</kbd> opens **Move to…** with recent folders first. <kbd>Space</kbd> peeks into a folder.
- **<kbd>?</kbd> on any item** shows its menu with each action's key.

### Fixes

- **Reschedule to…** opens its date picker at the item, not in the corner of the window.
- The task manager's menu button no longer covers its close button in themes such as Maple.
- Rescheduling from the task manager keeps the agenda's focus, on the next item.
- The selection glow and other accent tints follow the theme's accent colour.

## 0.6.0 · 3 November 2026 | Right where *you are.*

Alerts you can manage a task from, a page that shows where your keys are, and faster workspaces.

### Features

- **Agenda alerts as a task manager.** Reschedule to today, tomorrow, next week or a picked date; push back by any number of minutes; rename in the title; the item's menu and keys right in the alert; and its note unfurled inside.
- **Done on task alerts** marks the task done, as its checkbox would.
- **The picked-out card** shows an accent ring and a soft glow, and the card you last clicked into, which commands act on, a faint accent border.
- **Faster workspaces.** A page draws its cards side by side, so a slow card holds up no other.
- **Timer:** <kbd>Enter</kbd> in "What are you working on?" starts the timer, or resumes it.
- **Agenda timeline:** <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> zoom it when its card is picked out.

### Fixes

- Agenda times written with a narrow space before AM or PM, as some apps copy them, show at their time.
- A note card changed from outside, as by Claude in a Terminal card, keeps its cursor and vim mode.
- Terminal text follows a switch between light and dark mode.

## 0.5.0 · 2 November 2026 | Come *in.*

A tour for new arrivals, and a room to try Atrium Complete in.

### Features

- **Tutorial.** A fresh install opens a Welcome workspace with eight short notes on cards, stacks, notes, the agenda, keys and workspaces, beside live Agenda, Tasks and Files cards. **Open tutorial**, a command, on the page's right-click menu and in settings, brings it back.
- **The reading room.** Starting the trial opens a workspace with a fireplace behind the cards, the first chapter of *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*, its LibriVox audiobook on the Music card, Focus and Podcasts. Sample tasks, events and task notes come with it, so the agenda and tasks have something on them. **Remove sample data** deletes them and the room.
- **Click an agenda item or task** to bring up its alert, with Open note, Delete, Push back and Start now.
- **Files:** a File extensions setting; <kbd>f</kbd> on a row filters, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> in the filter goes to the top row.
- **Music:** a playlist note's web tracks show the cover picture beside the note.
- **Cards** start at 0.9 opacity with a little blur on new installs.

### Fixes

- Clicking a row in Files, folder or note, goes to it.
- Timed lines written without brackets, such as `- 9:30 PM Call`, show on the agenda. Only tasks and events alert, not notes.
- The outline on a task gone to is no longer cut off at the card's edge.

## 0.4.0 · 2 November 2026 | Never miss *a thing.*

The agenda tells you what's coming, and lets you act on it right there.

### Features

- **Notifications.** A system notification before a timed agenda item starts. Set how long before in Notify before, in each Agenda card's settings or the plugin's Agenda tab. Desktop only for now.
- **Alerts on screen.** Turn on Show agenda alerts on screen for a window with Start now, Push back 5 or 15 minutes, Remind me again, Dismiss and Stop notifying. An item not started comes back every 15 minutes, and once it's late its alert adds Cancel and Delete.
- **Press a on any agenda item** to bring up its alert window.
- **Starting a task** also marks it in progress.
- **New agenda items:** Tab goes through note, task, event and meeting, in that order.
- **Terminal** shows the card's background behind its text.

### Fixes

- Acting on an agenda item from its menu or alert keeps focus in the card, on the next item. Esc on the menu leaves it on the item.
- Agenda checkboxes are no longer clipped at the card's left edge.
- Change background > None in light mode shows no background.

## 0.3.1 · 1 November 2026 | Files, *your way.*

### Features

- **Look settings for Files.** Turn on file icons, bold folders, muted files and item counts, and set the gap between rows, from the card's settings.

## 0.3.0 · 1 November 2026 | Meet *Files.*

A new module: your vault's folders in a card, run from the keyboard.

### Features

- **[Files module](/files).** A folder view with a path filter, and keys for everything: Enter opens a note as a card, p peeks at it, → goes into a note unfurled in place and Esc comes back to its row.
- **Create, rename and delete.** New note and New folder sit at the foot of the card, reachable with the arrows. Deletes ask first, and Enter confirms.
- **A trail and a rail.** The title shows the folder's path, each part clickable. The rail slides in from the left with the folder's subfolders, and Go to folder jumps anywhere.
- **Move a card within its linked group** with Shift and the arrows.

### Fixes

- Notes unfurled inside a Vim-mode note show the text cursor and the insert-mode caret.

## 0.2.0 · 1 November 2026 | Faster *agenda.*

### Features

- **Add agenda items in plain words.** Type "meeting tomorrow at 9am" and the item goes there, with a hint of where it will land. Press n to add one, and Tab to change its kind.
- **Unscheduled notes.** An agenda note with no time shows on its day as unscheduled.
- **Rename cards** and **set the page's date format.**
- **Add a module from the room under a folded tile,** as at the foot of a column.
- **Music:** open files from the library, the cover and the history menus.
- **Mac Calendar events** count in the agenda title's next event, which moves on to the following one when it starts.
- **Stacks** show their count and tabs as soon as cards are stacked.

### Fixes

- Tiles stay still when you grab a border.
- Note cards keep in step with their note when lines are removed on disk.
- With many workspaces, the date stays on one line; their names scroll beside it, with the open one kept in sight.
- The Recorder writes one time for recordings under a minute.
