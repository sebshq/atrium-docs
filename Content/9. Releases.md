Title: Releases

# Releases | What's *new.*

Every version of Atrium, newest first, with what it added and what it fixed. Obsidian updates the plugin for you; Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates fetches a new one right away.

## 0.19.1 · 27 November 2026 | Stacks that expand alone, *borders that give.*

Fixes for expanded stacks, tile resizing and dated task notes.

### Fixes

- An expanded stack shows only the card showing, without the others beside it. ← →, } and {, or the Show next / previous card in stack commands switch it; give the command a hotkey to switch from inside a terminal or note.
- Dragging the line under a card in tiles takes room from the cards past a folded one, so a card above a folded card can grow.
- A task note's date property dates it when it has no due date.

## 0.19.0 · 26 November 2026 | Art for your backgrounds, *frames on every page.*

Art from Wikipedia for your backgrounds, frames in every view, and cards that live in the header.

### Features

- **Backgrounds from Wikipedia.** The [background](/backgrounds) picker searches Wikipedia as you type and shows paintings from Wikimedia Commons before you do. Art comes first; pick one and it downloads into your vault with its credit.
- **Filter by kind.** Art, Photos, Other, Videos and Untagged chips under the search show or hide each kind. Shift+1–5 toggles them, holding Shift shows their keys, and the picker remembers them.
- **A Backgrounds file.** One note lists what can be a background, wherever it is in your vault, with tags and where each came from. Typing a tag in the picker finds it; typing any name also finds the vault's other images.
- **Backgrounds manager.** Manage backgrounds… shows the list as a grid: set one as the background, edit its tags with autocomplete (t), take it off the list (Delete) or delete its file, and add images from the vault (n).
- **b changes the background.** From the page, and shown on the menu.
- **Frames.** Name a group of cards with Shift+E or Shift+Cmd-drag, in tiles, columns or the canvas, and go to it with g, ] and [, or Option+Shift and its number.
- **Header cards.** Drag a Calendar, Clock, Weather, Timer, Agenda or Tasks card into the header to keep its reading there; drag it back out, reorder it along the header, and make it Large or a Pill. The date is now a Calendar card; a click on a header card expands its card.
- **Canvas.** Resize cards from any edge or corner and push others aside; drop a card where you let go; double-click or press n for a new note card; with Snap to grid, Shift+arrows move a card one square. Card sizes can follow the zoom.

### Fixes

- The page's right-click menu opens in the middle of the window and stays there as you filter it.
- Esc with a menu open over an expanded card closes only the menu.
- The paragraph focus box moves with its lines as a note scrolls, and on a zoomed canvas.

## 0.18.0 · 25 November 2026 | Podcasts by show, *filters in sight.*

[Podcasts](/podcasts) is rebuilt around your shows, every card's filter sits above it, and settings work like cards.

### Features

- **Shows.** The [Podcasts](/podcasts) card opens on your shows' covers; click one and it travels up into a header over its episodes. **View as list** shows them as rows, each with its episode count and a way to unsubscribe.
- **Add a show.** The last card opens in place into a search with the cursor in it, filled with this week's most popular shows. Type to narrow them and find more, open one to try an episode before subscribing, and watch its cover glide in among yours when you do.
- **Up next, then everything unplayed.** Up next has its own tab, with every episode you haven't heard listed under what you've lined up; autoplay goes on down it. Drag an episode anywhere in Up next, its last place included.
- **Covers everywhere.** History and Up next show each episode's cover, even for shows you don't subscribe to.
- **Open episode website.** Every episode's menu opens its page on the show's site.
- **The filter in sight.** Every card that filters shows its filter bar above it. A chevron slides it away, and one near the card's top brings it back. Set **Filter** to **Smart show** to keep it tucked away as properties are, for every card or just one.
- **Settings like cards.** Settings dialogs have a filter and a tab for each section: left and right arrows move between them, <kbd>f</kbd> filters. They rise into the middle of the window and wear the focus borders.
- **Workspace settings with a key.** <kbd>,</kbd> on the page opens the workspace's settings beside it: its layout, the page, the background and the cards.

### Improvements

- **Marking played moves on.** Marking the episode playing as played starts the next one, with autoplay on.
- **Even rows.** Episode lists are evenly spaced, each cover level with its words.
- **A day's listening, expanded.** **Open daily note** on a History row opens the note right in the card.
- **Episode details.** Show notes is now **Episode details**.

### Fixes

- **No empty player.** With nothing playing, Podcasts shows no cover and no Transcript tab; while an episode plays, Transcript comes first.
- **Scrolling scrolls.** Scrolling over the Podcasts card no longer tucks its tabs or shrinks its cover; the buttons do that.
- **Readable first lines.** A transcript's, or lyrics', first and last lines no longer sit under the fade.

## 0.17.0 · 24 November 2026 | Routines to keep, *rooms to arrange.*

Routines play your morning one step at a time, Trends charts what your daily notes measure, and a workspace manager keeps your rooms in order.

### Features

- **[Routines](/routines).** A routine is a note and each step a line in it. Play one step by step, each on its own timer: click the timer to pause, add or take off a minute, take notes on a step, and play music from the [Music](/music) card while it runs. Each run goes into your daily note, so the [agenda](/agenda) shows it as one block holding its steps, and the card learns how long each step really takes; the history behind each suggested length is a note you can read and change. Schedule a routine like a task, or set when it must be done by and see when to start.
- **[Trends](/trends).** Habits is now Trends, with a **Chart** view beside Heatmap and Week: a line of any number property across your daily notes, such as weight, sleep or spending, over 3, 6 or 12 months.
- **[Workspace manager](/workspaces-and-layouts).** Every workspace as a picture of its page, to go to, rename in place, reorder by dragging or <kbd>⌥</kbd> + arrows, duplicate, delete and add, each by mouse and by key.
- **Switching on a click.** Click the workspace's name to slide the others out; they stay out a moment after the pointer leaves.

### Improvements

- **Faster workspaces.** Going to another workspace draws the page once and at once, about three times faster.
- **Cards glide between views.** Switching between tiles, columns and canvas slides each card to its new place.
- **Expanding on the canvas.** A canvas card expands over the dimmed page, as in tiles and columns, and the cards around it stay where they are.
- **Unstack.** Taking a card out of its stack is now **Unstack**, its key shown as <kbd>⌥;</kbd>, and listed with the shortcuts.

### Fixes

- **Focused events run.** A focused event with an end time still ahead gets the green running border.
- **The timeline keeps time.** The current time line moves with the clock, and the timeline keeps its place when the card is drawn again, as when you stop timing.
- **Menus narrow cleanly.** Typing in a menu leaves no blank rows.
- **Focus a Pomodoro log.** Focusing a finished Pomodoro starts that work again on a new line, keeping the log.
- **Canvas.** A folded card shows its whole title; a stack stays put whichever card shows, and an unstacked card goes beside it; switching from a canvas layout to columns no longer leaves cards overlapping.
- **Caret colour.** The text cursor follows a workspace's own colours, so it shows on a dark page.
- **Event notes.** An agenda event no longer gets two notes, and "1:1" is kept as "1-1" in note names.

## 0.16.0 · 20 November 2026 | Spread out, *find your way.*

A Canvas view lays your cards out on an endless canvas, a Graph card maps notes and their links, and a new background changes colour as you move around.

### Features

- **Canvas view.** A third layout beside [tiles and columns](/tiles-and-columns): each card has a place and a size of its own on a canvas that fills the window. Grab empty space or scroll to pan; pinch or <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + scroll to zoom; <kbd>Option</kbd> + arrows pan and <kbd>Option</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + up and down zoom. Drag a card's frame to move it and its edges to resize it; the cards in its way move aside, and cards never overlap. Expanding a card grows it in place. Each workspace remembers where you were looking.
- **Find your way on the canvas.** Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to see a marker at the edge for each card out of sight, with its number; click it or press <kbd>Shift</kbd> + its number to go there. **Go to card…** (<kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>) finds one by name, <kbd>Option</kbd> + <kbd>0</kbd> goes back to actual size, and **Zoom to fit** shows everything.
- **Snap to grid and Tidy up.** Cards snap to a grid unless you turn it off; **Tidy up** snaps every card and moves apart any that touch.
- **Move several cards at once.** <kbd>Shift</kbd>- or <kbd>Cmd</kbd>-click cards, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>-drag a box across empty space, then drag any of them or press <kbd>Shift</kbd> + arrows.
- **Add a module where you are.** Double-click empty space, or hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to see the **Add module** box, placed where the card will fit, and <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click it.
- **Graph card.** A map of a note and its links, each note in a place of its own. Click a note to walk to it, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click to open it alongside; open notes make room around them and can be edited in place. Arrows move between notes, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens one or goes into it, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> comes back out.
- **Position gradient background.** A colour that shifts with where you are on the canvas: each direction turns it a different way, and the farther you go, the more it changes. Pick the starting colour in Appearance settings, and **Set background home here** from the page menu.

### Fixes

- **No sideways scrolling in task lists.** [Tasks](/tasks), [Agenda](/agenda) and [Focus](/focus) no longer shift a few pixels sideways when scrolled.
- **Folded task groups close up.** A folded group in [Tasks](/tasks) is just its heading again, with no gap below.
- **Focus rings.** Whatever has the keys wears the large ring: a card picked out, or the note or field you're typing in.
- **Smoother cards over a gradient.** Card blur no longer flickers in patches over the Position gradient.

## 0.15.0 · 18 November 2026 | Cards to learn, *breaks to take.*

Memorize learns cards as well as passages, the Pomodoro takes breaks, the Focus card finds what's related, and tasks can be drawn as in a bullet journal.

### Features

- **Cards in [Memorize](/memorize).** A `cue :: answer` line is a card, and `:::` makes it work both ways. An image note is one too, its properties the answer, such as a painting's title, artist and year. Phrases and their translations work the same way. Cards go through Learn, Practice and Perfect; every stage, of cards and passages, is passed by first letters, whole words, then aloud.
- **Log practice to the daily note.** A Memorize setting adds a timed line, such as `- [3:40 PM] Memorized [[John 3.16]]`, for each session.
- **Related notes on the [Focus](/focus) card.** Backlinks, outlinks and unlinked mentions of the focused item, each addable as a reference. **Focus on…** (<kbd>f</kbd>) finds a task or note to focus on.
- **Breaks in the [Pomodoro](/pomodoro).** **Take a break** after focus, a window to get back to work when the break is up, and the minutes set by clicking the time.
- **Manage recordings.** The [Recorder](/recorder) can discard a recording, delete one, and list them all to play or delete.
- **Change an item's time.** Start and End, or <kbd>t</kbd>, in an item's manager.
- **Bullet journal markers.** An Appearance setting draws tasks and events as in a bullet journal: • to do, / in progress, × done, > migrated, ○ an event.
- **Straight to done.** A [Tasks](/tasks) setting, **Clicking a task's checkbox**, can skip in progress. In progress stays on the right-click menu.
- **A moment to see it done.** A finished task shows its tick under the confetti before it leaves the list, and an Appearance setting turns the confetti off.

### Fixes

- **One header for readings.** Folded or small, [Weather](/weather), [Clock](/clock) and the Pomodoro show their name at the left and their reading at the right, and no longer centre one or the other.
- **Weather saves itself.** **Save to daily notes automatically** now fills a daily note whose `weather` property is there but empty.
- **Terminal colours.** A [Terminal](/terminal) takes each workspace's colours when you switch.
- **Seconds on the Clock.** The folded Clock shows them when they're on, and the Timer no longer repeats its name.

## 0.14.0 · 17 November 2026 | Learn it, *then say it.*

Memorize gets more stages, a spoken pass at each one and a pause to celebrate between them, the Focus card can finish or put off what it shows, and a fresh install opens on a new Welcome workspace.

### Features

- **More stages in [Memorize](/memorize).** After Half hidden come **Mostly hidden**, with two of every three words cut to their first letter, and **Line starts**, with only the first word of each line or sentence showing, before All hidden and the test.
- **A spoken pass at each stage.** Pass a stage typed, then say it aloud to open the next. **Skip spoken** moves on without it, and the card's **Spoken passes** setting turns them off.
- **A pause between stages.** A passed stage shows its score and when the next is best started, 10 minutes on. **Go on now** starts it at once, **Later** waits for it under **Coming up**, and a passed stage's chip takes you back to it.
- **Reveal a hidden word.** Click a hidden word to see it; it counts as missed.
- **Stop memorizing.** On a passage's right-click menu, or as a command, it leaves the lists and keeps its progress. **Show stopped passages**, on the card's menu, lists them to **Resume**.
- **Passages on the Tasks card.** Passages due to practise show at the top of [Tasks](/tasks), practised with a click, deferred like a task.
- **Done, Defer and Unfocus on the [Focus](/focus) card.** Under the item it shows, finish it, put it off or stop focusing on it. A labelled Pomodoro session shows there too.
- **Later today.** **Defer to later today (+4 hours)** joins the defer menus of Tasks and Focus.
- **A Welcome workspace to learn on.** A fresh install opens the tutorial on *The Starry Night*, with a note whose links open in place, the lessons in a card to step through with <kbd>]</kbd>, and Tasks and Files stacked.

### Fixes

- **Faster speech.** Spoken words are heard about a second after they're said, the microphone starts sooner, and later recitations no longer lose their first words.
- **Nothing to practise right now.** Memorize says so while a passage comes back later today, and counts down to it under **Coming up**.
- **Stacks stay open.** A stack's card names stay out a moment after the pointer leaves the header, as the workspaces do.
- **Pomodoro label.** Clearing it leaves the cursor in the field.
- **Add a module.** The hint shows <kbd>:</kbd> to stack.

## 0.13.0 · 13 November 2026 | Learn it *by heart.*

A new card for learning passages word for word, from your notes, with reviews spaced out as you learn them.

### Features

- **[Memorize](/memorize).** Make any note, or any paragraph tagged `#memorize`, a passage to learn. You learn it in stages: read it, then with every other word cut to its first letter, then every word, then a test. Answer by typing each word's first letter, typing whole words, or saying it aloud on macOS 26 or later. A run of 90% or better opens the next stage. A passed test is scheduled with FSRS, the scheduler Anki uses, so reviews come further apart as you know it. Long notes are learned in pieces of about 40 words, broken at headings and horizontal rules, each led into by the sentence before it. Part of [Atrium Complete](/atrium-complete).

## 0.12.0 · 13 November 2026 | One focus, *many filters.*

Focus becomes the one way to time work, every filter takes several terms at once, and an expanded stack shows its other cards to flip to.

### Features

- **Several filters in one.** In any card's filter, `gym; run` shows either, `gym & run` shows lines with both, and `gym & -draft` leaves out the drafts. Put text in "quotes" to search for it as written. Task words work as terms too: `todo & gym`.
- **Focus is the one way to time work.** Focusing a task or agenda item times it, and focusing another ends it. Start and Focus are one button now, and focusing something else ends a running Pomodoro. The Focus card adds **Done and unfocus** (<kbd>d</kbd>), and **Unfocus and stop tracking** ends the time and takes the tag off. Old `#tracking` lines are rewritten as `#focus`.
- **Order Files by a property.** Pick a property in a Files card's settings and the files that have it come first, in its order, then the rest by name. **Property values** shows each file's value at the right of its row.
- **Flip through a stack.** <kbd>Space</kbd> on a stack expands its front card with the stack's other cards folded beside it. Click one, or press <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, and the cards turn to bring it forward.
- **Space opens the item manager.** On a task or agenda item, <kbd>Space</kbd> opens its manager instead of expanding the card.
- **Waveform colour.** Themes and snippets can set the Music card's waveform with `--atrium-waveform-h`, `--atrium-waveform-s` and `--atrium-waveform-l`. It follows the accent colour by default.
- **Files opens with a click.** A click on a note opens it in one card that the Files card reuses, so browsing doesn't fill the page; <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click opens a new card. A click on a folder goes into it, and the chevron unfolds it in place.

### Fixes

- A note card no longer saves its text over a change written to the note just after, which could lose a Pomodoro's 🍅 line.
- The reschedule date picker opens beside its item and keeps the day you pick. **Pick a date** starts on the task's due date, and **Reschedule to…** on the item's day.
- Expanding a card leaves its place on the page, so the cards behind it don't move or reload.
- A folded Monthly card shows its month.
- The item manager's title shows text without markdown marks.
- A reminder lists **Remind me again** above **Push back**.
- A card shown from its stack, such as the Agenda behind the daily card, draws whole instead of staying folded to its header.
- Atrium no longer reads files or environment details outside Obsidian's own API, and the README lists the programs it runs on your computer.

The Files card gets the actions you reach for most, and browser cards keep their page.

### Features

- **Pull for the filter.** Pull the Files list down past its top to reveal the filter, and scroll down to tuck it away again.
- **Big clocks fit.** In a big tile the Clock stacks its time and date so they stay on screen, and adds the week of the year.

### Fixes

- Browser cards keep their page as cards are added, moved, expanded or floated, and on undo, instead of loading it again.
- Terminal text stays readable in light workspaces, and follows a workspace's light or dark mode as it changes.
- Settings changed from a card's own menu, such as Show macOS calendar events, are no longer forgotten after cancelling a settings dialog.

## 0.10.0 · 9 November 2026 | Send it to *another day.*

Move lines to another day, week or month, and keep two days open side by side.

### Features

- **Menus and dialogs take a workspace's own light or dark mode,** when it differs from Obsidian's.

### Fixes

- Dialogs over Atrium no longer fade the page behind them.
- The Focus card's Add reference field has no dark band around it.
- Podcasts: back and forward sit beside play at every card size, and the buttons over the cover stay visible on hover.
- A card's editor shows an undo while it still has the cursor.
