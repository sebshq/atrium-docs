Title: Working with notes

# Working with notes | Follow a thought *without leaving the page.*

Notes in Atrium do a few things Obsidian's don't. A link opens its note right under the line, ready to edit. A filter narrows any note to the lines you want. And the clutter around a note, its properties and backlinks, waits out of sight until you reach for it.

## Links that open in place | Click a link. *The note unfolds.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/notes-links.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/notes-links.jpg"></video>

Click a `[[link]]` in a note card (in live preview or source view), or the small arrow that shows beside it on hover, and the linked note opens right under its line. It isn't a preview: you can type in it, tick its tasks, follow its own links, and every change saves to that note. Click the link again to fold it away.

Point at the opened note and four buttons show in its corner: **Close**, **Open** in a tab, **Open as card**, which gives it a card of its own beside the page, and **Delete note**.

Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> on Windows) as you click to open the link the usual way instead.

## Filter | Just the lines *you came for.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/notes-filter.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/notes-filter.jpg"></video>

Press <kbd>f</kbd> on a card, or click the funnel in its header, and type. Only the paragraphs, list items and tasks that match stay in view, the matches lit up, the rest folded into small dots. It's still the note, so you can edit what's left. Click the dots, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to see the whole note again.

- **todo** or **task** shows the open tasks; **done** shows the finished ones.
- **Preserve structure** keeps the headings above each match, so you can see where it lives.
- The funnel at the bar's left, or a leading <kbd>-</kbd>, flips it round to hide the matches instead, to clear away what you've dealt with.
- The filter reaches into the notes opened under their links, and into embedded notes.

The filter stays with the card until you clear it, even after Obsidian restarts. Agenda, Tasks, Recent files, Music and Podcasts cards filter too, by item.

## Tucked away | Properties and backlinks, *when you want them.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/notes-tucked.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/notes-tucked.jpg"></video>

A note card keeps its properties tucked above the first line: scroll up past the top, or use the small handle that shows on hover, and they slide down. Its backlinks wait below the end in a drawer: keep scrolling past the last line and it opens.

Each card can show its note in live preview, source or reading view, with properties shown, hidden or tucked; it's on the card's right-click menu.

## Into a card | Drag a preview *onto the page.*

<video src="/_assets/guide/notes-drag.mp4" poster="/_assets/guide/notes-drag.jpg"></video>

Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> on Windows) and point at a link in a note card: its note pops up, editable. In columns, drag the pop-up by its title over the page and the gaps between cards light up; let go over one and the note becomes a card there.
